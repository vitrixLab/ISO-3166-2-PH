Executive Summary

This project aims to integrate ISO 3166-2:PH codes into legal databases to improve organization and retrieval of information concerning the principal subdivisions of the Philippines. The documentation describes the project's objectives, implementation direction, and significance in the legal domain.

Objectives

  1. Database Integration:
    • Incorporate ISO 3166-2:PH codes into existing legal databases.
    • Provide a consistent structure for subdivision and jurisdiction data.
  2. User Training:
    • Develop materials explaining the use and implications of ISO 3166-2:PH codes.
  3. Documentation:
    • Document the standard, code structure, data model, and usage guidelines.

Why it matters

  • Improved organization and categorization of legal information.
  • More efficient retrieval of jurisdiction-related data.
  • Better consistency and collaboration across legal-data workflows.
